Get the addon

Journey Tracker records your climb from level 1 to 60 as you play. It's free, and nothing leaves the game until you paste it here.

Download Journey Tracker __ADDON_VERSION__ · zip · __DOWNLOAD_SIZE__
  1. Unzip it into your WoW Forever AddOns folder, so you end up with World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\JourneyTracker.
  2. Restart the game. Type /journey or click the gold JT button on the minimap to see your stats.
  3. Play. It records as you go: the road you took, every death and quest, kills, gold and more.
  4. For your recap, click Export in its window (or right-click the minimap button), press Ctrl+C and paste it in the box here.
  5. Every ten levels it also keeps your journey as it was then. Pick At 30 in the export window (or type /journey export 30) to share your road to 30.

Saving your export counts it toward the rankings and gives your recap a link you can share. Exports never include your character's name or realm.

A saved journey is ranked among the others at its milestone, every ten levels: a level 34 journey among the level 30 ones, on its time to 30, with each other character's export closest to 30. One exported at its milestone's level more than two hours of play after reaching it is ranked only on the time it took and the levels it did things at, since everything else kept growing after. It's ranked on the rankings that apply to it, among all players or its own class, race, faction or realm type. You see the ones you stand out in most. A place shows once 3 journeys are on a ranking, like "2nd of 9", and becomes a share like "Top 3%" at 100. The sample journey's places are made up, and so are the numbers it doesn't have.
